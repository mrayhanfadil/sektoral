# Per-share figures use one reviewed share ledger

Every per-share figure in a Company Update (value per share, market capitalization, EPS, BVPS, DPS, PER) divides by one count: the Share Ledger's Report Date count, which is the latest official register count on or before the Report Date (outstanding, net of treasury shares) moved by completed corporate actions known by then. Consecutive register counts must reconcile through the ledger's dated actions, or the ledger is incomplete and the model falls back to the official balance-sheet count with that gap named. EPS in every forecast year uses this pro forma count so PER stays comparable across years; the IAS 33 / PSAK 56 weighted average for the first forecast year is recorded beside it for reconciliation to the issuer's annual EPS, and an issuer figure that does not reconcile to dated actions is flagged rather than used. Warrants dilute the target price only when the value per share exceeds their exercise price. First-year Core Earnings come from the reviewed normalization bridge and feed P/E and P/B-ROE; the income statement stays as reported. We chose one ledger over per-method share reads because issued counts, stale cache counts and interim counts had silently mixed, putting treasury shares in the denominator.
