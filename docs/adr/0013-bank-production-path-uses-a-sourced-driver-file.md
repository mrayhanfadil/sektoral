# The bank production path uses a sourced driver file

A bank forecast is Production-Ready only when `app.bank_model` runs on a sourced bank driver file (`data/bank_drivers/<TICKER>.json`): official 31 December and 30 June balances in one definition, the official first-half income lines (no inferred cost split), the regulatory CAR requirement the bank discloses (risk-profile minimum plus conservation, countercyclical and systemic buffers) as the capital floor instead of its lowest historical CAR, a sourced cost of funds and payout policy, and five driver rows labelled as company guidance, sourced ratios or analyst assumptions. The model must balance, roll equity and dividends forward and hold the floor; the DDM values its dividends with the terminal payout at 1 - g / ROE capped by the policy payout; the terminal passes its economics check; `app.reference_ddm` rebuilds the profit path in closed form and agrees within 0.5% (a model that applied a capital or funding constraint is not compared and cannot pass); and the house policy must be effective on the Report Date. Bank checks never inherit FCFF, capex, working-capital or WACC requirements.
